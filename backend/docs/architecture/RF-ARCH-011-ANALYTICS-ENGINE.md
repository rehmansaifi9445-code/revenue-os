# RevenueFlow Analytics Engine

Version: 1.0

Status: Foundation Approved

---

# Purpose

The Analytics Engine transforms raw business data into meaningful insights.

It measures business performance, detects trends, supports AI learning and provides intelligence for decision making.

The Analytics Engine never changes business data.

It only analyzes and reports.

---

# Vision

Every business owner should understand the health of their business in seconds.

Analytics should not only describe the past but also help predict the future.
