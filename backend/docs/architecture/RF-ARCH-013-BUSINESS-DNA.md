# RevenueFlow Business DNA

Version: 1.0

Status: Foundation Approved

---

# Purpose

Every business is unique.

RevenueFlow must adapt to every business instead of forcing every business to follow the same process.

Business DNA is the digital personality of a business.

It defines how RevenueFlow behaves for that specific business.

---

# Vision

RevenueFlow should never treat two businesses exactly the same.

Every business has its own recovery style, communication style, risk tolerance and workflow.

Business DNA allows RevenueFlow to understand and respect those differences.

---

# What is Business DNA?

Business DNA is a collection of business preferences, behaviour patterns, policies and intelligence that make one business different from another.

# DNA Learning

Business DNA is not static.

It evolves over time.

RevenueFlow continuously learns from business behaviour and suggests improvements.

Business owners always approve important DNA changes.

---

# Company Rules

Every business has its own DNA.

Business DNA never changes automatically without approval.

AI learns from DNA.
