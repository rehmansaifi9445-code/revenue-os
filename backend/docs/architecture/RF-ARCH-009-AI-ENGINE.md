# RevenueFlow AI Engine

Version: 1.0

Status: Foundation Approved

---

# Purpose

The AI Engine is the intelligence layer of RevenueFlow.

It does not replace business rules.

It continuously observes, learns, predicts, recommends, and optimizes business operations.

The AI Engine never directly modifies business data.

All actions must pass through the Event Engine, Rule Engine and Automation Engine.

---

# Vision

Build the world's smartest AI-powered Revenue Recovery Intelligence Platform.

The AI Engine must continuously reduce payment recovery time, increase recovery success rate, reduce manual work, and improve customer relationships.

---

# AI Core

Master Intelligence Core

The Master Intelligence Core coordinates all intelligence modules.

It decides which intelligence module should be used for each situation.

# AI Principles

AI never bypasses business rules.

AI never bypasses security.

AI never executes actions directly.

AI always explains recommendations.

AI continuously improves itself.

AI decisions must be auditable.

---

# Company Rules

Every AI recommendation must be explainable.

Every AI prediction must include confidence.

Every AI action must be traceable.

Every AI interaction must generate an audit record.

Business owner always has final control.
